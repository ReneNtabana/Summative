About the model and its limitations

10 topics: politics, sport, economy, health, education, entertainment, relationship, technology, environment, culture. Trained on 1,219 de-duplicated articles from eight Burundian news websites (KIRNEWS; Niyongabo et al., 2020). Held-out test: accuracy 0.90, macro-F1 0.80. On 1,525 BBC Gahuza articles (MasakhaNEWS): accuracy 0.87.

  • Rare topics (culture, environment, technology) are less reliable.
  • The model is over-confident, so predictions below 90% confidence are flagged.
  • It always picks one of the 10 topics. Other topics (e.g. religion) usually come out as politics.
  • This page runs an int8-quantised copy of the model. It agrees with the full model on 95% of test articles.
